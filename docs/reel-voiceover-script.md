## How to record it

Your own voice is the right call here, and not just because it's free. A founder
recruiting pilot restaurants is more persuasive than a polished stock read — it
sounds like a person who built the thing, which is exactly what you are. The
synthetic voices on this Mac are the basic set and sound it; the paid voice
service has a zero balance.

- **On the Mac:** QuickTime Player → File → New Audio Recording. Or Voice Memos.
- **Mic distance:** about a hand's width, slightly off to the side so plosives
  ("point", "pilot") don't thump.
- **Room:** somewhere soft — a room with a rug, curtains, a sofa. Avoid the
  kitchen and the bathroom; hard surfaces give you slap-back echo I can't remove.
- **Takes:** record each line separately with a two-second pause either side.
  Do each one two or three times; I'll pick the best and sync them to frame.
- **Delivery:** talk to one restaurant owner across a table, not to an audience.
  Drop your energy about 20% from where you think it should be.
- **Don't** over-enunciate "No contract." Let it sit flat and land.
